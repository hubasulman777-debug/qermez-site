Edition V · The Studio

Made slowly

TheQermez was started in Lahore by one woman and has grown into a small team. It began with a simple feeling: the games we grew up with, Ludo on the floor and snakes and ladders with cousins, deserved to travel with us again.

Every board is printed or painted by hand and finished with a single red stitch, our maker’s mark. The name comes from qirmiz, crimson, the colour that runs through everything we make. Some pieces are personal: Naqsh began with a pattern from the founder’s grandmother’s chadar.

Made slowly, for unhurried afternoons.

Notes for Collectors

Ordering & Delivery

Made to order. Dispatched in 3–7 working days. Delivered across Pakistan and worldwide.

How to order +

Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram with the piece you’d like. We’ll confirm availability, your total and dispatch date before you pay.

Payment: bank transfer, in advance. We share account details when we confirm your order and dispatch once payment is received.

Dispatch & delivery +

Every bag is made to order by hand. Most pieces are dispatched within 3 to 7 working days, depending on the design. Bespoke pieces take 7–10 working days after mockup approval.

We deliver to every city in Pakistan and ship worldwide. Delivery charges depend on where your order is going; we’ll confirm the exact amount before you pay. International customers pay any customs duties or taxes charged in their country.

Exchanges & returns +

Because every bag is made for you, we don’t accept returns for a change of mind.

If your order arrives damaged or isn’t what you ordered, message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We’ll replace it or refund you in full, including delivery.

Small variations in brushwork, ink and colour are part of handmade work, not defects.

Cancellations: free within 24 hours of ordering, before we begin. Bespoke orders can’t be cancelled once the mockup is approved.

Canvas care +
  • Spot clean where you can, especially hand-painted Studies.
  • To wash, turn inside out and hand wash in cold water with mild detergent.
  • No bleach, no wringing, no tumble dryer. Dry flat in the shade.
  • Iron on the reverse, on low.
  • Keep the pieces dry and store them in the potli.
