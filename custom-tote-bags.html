Custom Tote Bags, made by hand

A sketch, a name, a line of calligraphy. Send it to us and we’ll make it into a tote bag in our Lahore studio, finished with our single red stitch.

Try your artwork on a tote → WhatsApp us

How it works

  • Upload your sketch or image in our Try on Tote studio.
  • Place it on the canvas: move, scale and rotate it until it looks right. The studio can remove a white paper background for you.
  • Send it to us on WhatsApp straight from the studio, or download the mockup to keep.
  • Approve the final mockup and price. We make your bag in 7–10 working days.

Ideas

Your child’s drawing. A name or initials in Urdu or English calligraphy. A line of poetry you love. A family pattern, like the chadar that inspired our Naqsh tote. A gift for a wedding, a birthday or someone far from home.

Every custom tote is 38 × 42 cm cotton canvas. See how to care for it.

Questions

What can I put on a custom tote? +
A sketch, a drawing, a name, a line of calligraphy or a design of your own. Upload it in our Try on Tote studio to see how it looks, then send it to us.
How long does a custom tote take? +
Bespoke pieces take 7–10 working days after you approve the mockup.
How much does a custom tote cost? +
It depends on the design. Send us your artwork on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm the price before anything is made.
Can I cancel a custom order? +
Orders can be cancelled free within 24 hours of ordering, before we begin. Bespoke orders can’t be cancelled once the mockup is approved.
Do you ship custom totes abroad? +
Yes. We deliver across Pakistan and worldwide. Delivery charges are confirmed before you pay.
