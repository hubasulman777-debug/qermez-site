The Studio · Lahore

Made slowly

TheQermez was started in Lahore by one woman and has grown into a small team. It began with a simple feeling: the games we grew up with, Ludo on the floor and snakes and ladders with cousins, deserved to travel with us again.

Every board is printed or painted by hand and finished with a single red stitch, our maker’s mark. The name comes from qirmiz, crimson, the colour that runs through everything we make.

Some pieces are personal: Naqsh began with a pattern from the founder’s grandmother’s chadar. Others carry the causes we stand with, like Wahda and Azadi.

Made slowly, for unhurried afternoons.

One red stitch

A single bar tack in cochineal red at the left handle. Our maker’s mark, on every bag.

The potli

Every Games Edit tote comes with a drawstring pouch for its pieces, so the game always travels with the bag.

The card

A provenance card on 300gsm cotton rag tells each design’s story and, for the games, the rules of play.
