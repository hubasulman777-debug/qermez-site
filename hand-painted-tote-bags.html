About the Studies

Every Study is 38 × 42 cm, painted by hand and finished with our maker’s mark: a single red stitch at the left handle. Because each bag is painted again for each order, no two are exactly alike. Small differences in brushwork and colour are part of handmade work.

Studies are made to order and usually dispatched within 3 to 7 working days, to anywhere in Pakistan or worldwide. Want something of your own? See our custom tote bags.

Care

  • The bag is washable. Turn it inside out before washing.
  • Hand wash in cold water with a mild detergent. No bleach, no wringing, no tumble dryer.
  • For hand-painted Studies, spot clean where you can.
  • Don’t iron or press directly on the bag.
  • If the canvas shrinks a little after washing, lay it flat, place some weight on it and let it dry. It will settle back into shape.
  • Dry flat in the shade. Keep the game pieces dry and stored in their potli.

Questions

How do I order? +
Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram with the piece you’d like. We confirm availability, your total and dispatch date, then you pay by bank transfer in advance and we start making your bag.
How long does it take? +
Every bag is made to order by hand. Most pieces are dispatched within 3 to 7 working days. Bespoke pieces take 7–10 working days after mockup approval.
Do you ship outside Pakistan? +
Yes. We deliver to every city in Pakistan and ship worldwide. Delivery charges depend on the destination and are confirmed before you pay. International customers pay any customs duties or taxes charged in their country.
Can I return it? +
Because every bag is made for you, we don’t accept returns for a change of mind. If your order arrives damaged or isn’t what you ordered, message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we’ll replace it or refund you in full, including delivery.
